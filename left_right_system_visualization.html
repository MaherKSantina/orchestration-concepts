Core guiding principles
AI at the edges, logic in the core
AI handles fuzziness; deterministic logic handles correctness and repeatability.
Extract functions, not just examples
Move from collecting outputs to discovering reusable, composable abstractions.
Structured variation under evaluation
Change controllable levers, isolate effects, observe differences, test claims.
Tools accelerate. Mind extracts.
Automation speeds exploration; function extraction and abstraction remain cognitive.
Progressive formalization
Leave uncertain areas exploratory. Formalize only what proves stable and repeatable.
Own the what & why. Rent the how.
Build your ontology, evaluators, and success criteria. Let platforms own generic runtimes.
Process as a first-class object
Processes have goals, inputs, outputs, states, artifacts, evaluators — reusable across domains.
